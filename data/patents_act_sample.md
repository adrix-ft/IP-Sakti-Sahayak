# The Patents Act, 1970

## Section 3: What are not inventions
The following are not inventions within the meaning of this Act:

### Section 3(p)
An invention which in effect is traditional knowledge or which is an aggregation or duplication of known properties of traditionally known component or components.

- **Jurisdiction:** India
- **Act Name:** The Patents Act, 1970
- **Section:** 3(p)
- **Enforcing Agency:** Intellectual Property India (CGPDTM) / Ministry of Commerce and Industry
- **Ayush Relevance:** Prohibits patenting known Ayurvedic formulations, medicinal plants, or traditional remedies unless there is substantial novel efficacy demonstrated.
